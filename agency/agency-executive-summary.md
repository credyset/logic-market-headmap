# Logic Agency Market Headmap — Executive Summary

**Date:** 2026-09-01  
**Scope:** 6,854 classified chunks from 332 external Startup CPG sources; 17 retained Agency-relevant topics. No Logic first-party material is included.

## What the Agency evidence emphasizes

Retail buyer readiness, wholesale expansion, in-store velocity, channel economics, retailer onboarding, and regional expansion dominate the evidence. Co-manufacturer scale-up, inventory planning, gross margin discipline, and operational risk form the execution layer underneath retail growth.

The strongest recent movement is in gross-margin discipline, operational risk/contracts, AI-enabled operations, product development, broker management, and co-manufacturer scale-up. These are trend signals in the corpus—not forecasts.

## Commercial read

Logic Agency should speak to the operating gap between winning retail and staying operationally healthy: bottoms-up forecasting, supplier and co-man control, broker/distributor coordination, retailer compliance, and inventory/cash discipline. The market language is operator-led and problem-specific; content should preserve that specificity.

## Evidence note

Guest share is higher than host share across all retained topics (roughly 60–73%), so the language map prioritizes founder/operator pain and learning rather than host framing. CSV outputs were schema-validated after correcting the trend-quarter alignment.
