# Aligned category expansion — Logic Agency and Logic Pac

**Last verified:** 2026-09-02  
**Confidence:** Medium-high for directly linked transcript and publisher sources; exploratory for babytech and headphones, where category-native transcript coverage remains thin.

## What this pass adds

This is an additive evidence layer. It does not alter the historical topic-frequency denominator. It expands the commercial frame into luxury goods, jewelry, elevated DTC packaging, influencer kits, consumer electronics, headphones, babytech, and adjacent hardware.

## Logic Agency signals

- **Retail readiness in hardware is operational.** Consumer-electronics brands need channel economics, packaging/display readiness, forecasts, inventory, distributor coordination, and the ability to support the retailer after the first order.
- **Luxury presentation creates operating obligations.** Layered structures, special finishes, inserts, and personalized components add suppliers, lead time, assembly, quality-control, freight, and version-control complexity.
- **Electronics and babytech raise the consequence of packaging failure.** Protection, setup clarity, safety cues, theft/display requirements, returns, and damage costs are linked rather than separate workstreams.
- **Elevated brands need a packaging maturity path.** A launch or first-order experience may justify more elaborate packaging than replenishment. The operating system must distinguish campaign packaging from scalable repeat-order packaging.

## Logic Pac signals

- **The package must substantiate the price.** Jewelry, fragrance, luxury beauty, and premium electronics use material, structure, sound, tactility, pacing, and reveal to make the product feel coherent with its price point.
- **Unboxing is a sequence.** Outer shipper, opening message, insert, product pack, and final reveal each have a job; this is particularly important for influencer and PR kits.
- **Premium cannot ignore delivery.** Theft visibility, transit protection, pack-out labor, cube, returns, and repeat-order costs can undermine an otherwise impressive design.
- **Influencer kits are media infrastructure.** The practical system includes creator-specific presentation, secure nesting, campaign information, kitting, shipping, tracking, and an intentional moment worth filming.

## High-fit content and outreach questions

1. Does the packaging make the product feel worth its price before the customer uses it?
2. Is the unboxing designed for the shelf, the doorstep, the camera—or all three?
3. What changes between launch packaging, influencer kits, first orders, and repeat orders?
4. Can the structure protect the product without inflating cube, damage, or fulfillment labor?
5. Does the packaging make setup, safety, authenticity, and returns easier for electronics or babytech?
6. Can the current supplier and quality system reproduce the premium details at scale?
