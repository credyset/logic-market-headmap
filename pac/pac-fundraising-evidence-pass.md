# Logic Pac — Fundraising Evidence Pass

**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Purpose:** Test whether packaging evidence supports a fundraising/investor-readiness lens.

## Finding

The Pac-specific corpus does not contain enough direct investor or founder fundraising discussion to claim that packaging is a primary fundraising trigger. The defensible conclusion is narrower: packaging decisions can affect the investment case through margin, scale, compliance, supply-chain resilience, brand equity, and sustainability risk.

| Investor-specific demand | No direct investor interview or fundraising decision in the clean Pac set. | Mark as gap; collect dedicated investor/board/finance sources. | Internal corpus audit |

## Recommended next collection

Collect 10–15 direct sources from beauty investors, CFOs, brand finance leaders, and founders discussing packaging budgets, launch readiness, gross margin, working capital, EPR exposure, and packaging redesign decisions. Keep these separate from editorial trend sources so investor language is not inferred from supplier marketing.

## Guardrail

Do not state that packaging “drives fundraising” based on this pass. State only that packaging can enter diligence through economics, scale, risk, and brand defensibility.
