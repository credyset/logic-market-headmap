Five decision lenses

Manufacturing: sourcing, production, cost, MOQ, and scale. Design: identity, visual language, sensory cues, luxury, and unboxing. Product categories: beauty-led evidence, especially skincare; not sufficient to generalize across every Pac category. Retail success: shelf fit, category cues, conversion, and premium positioning. Fundraising: direct evidence is thin and requires a dedicated investor/brand-finance source pass.

How to use this

Counts are corpus signals, not market size. Use the themes to prioritize questions, content, and sales conversations; validate commercial decisions against Logic’s own operating experience and current client data.
