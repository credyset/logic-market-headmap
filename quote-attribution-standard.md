# Podcast quote attribution standard

Direct podcast quotations may appear in the public Heatmap only when the record contains:

1. Speaker name
2. Speaker company
3. Speaker job title at the time of the episode
4. Podcast name
5. Episode title
6. Direct episode or transcript URL
7. Verification status identifying a full transcript or publisher-provided excerpt

Records missing any field remain internal research excerpts. Insights derived from those records may be published only as clearly labeled editorial paraphrases without implied attribution.

The outreach one-liners in `outreach-painpoint-insights.csv` are hypotheses for message development. They are not verbatim quotes and should be tested against segment, account context, and current market evidence before use.
